A New Perspective

Say you have an idea. You want to create something. But you have very few means. You have maybe some money to buy materials, and create a website, but beyond that, what can you do.

With network neutrality, creativity thrives. It gives everyone-even people like me and you, the ability to create, work hard, and thrive to do more in the world. I can create a website, and expect that the website will be visited. My assumption is that anyone who wants to will be able access my website, just like they access any other website on the world.

Suddenly, Network Neutrality is no longer a thing. Now I have to pay for my website to get visited, to load. What is this? I can no longer be innovative. And if I am, I have to pay. This turns away teens and young people with little means from being able to do anything. Without equal access to the internet, people with fresh new ideas will get the impression that their ideas aren’t wanted. They will stop creating, stop inventing, and join the rest of the world as mindless robots.

Without the proper innovation, our world will become a desolate wasteland. If we have no young minds, thinking, creating, and sharing their ideas with the world, our world will stay exactly where we are right now. No small new companies will emerge creating the next great era of technology. The people who have to be targeted, are the young creative minds. Without network neutrality, the young minds will be stamped out, they will be forced into submission, and nothing will come out of it.

We live in a great era of technology. For the first time, adolescents have the chance to change the world, make a difference with their minds, simultaneously doing what they love. But this can only happen with network neutrality. Keep teens involved
